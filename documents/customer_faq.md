# Noor Fashion – Customer FAQ (internal copy)

Noor Fashion is a fictional Dubai-based occasion-wear brand used for this demo.

## Returns and exchanges

Customers can return any item within 14 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have the original tags attached. Sale items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned, but can be exchanged for a different size within 7 days. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the item reaches our warehouse. Customers who paid cash on delivery receive their refund as store credit or by bank transfer.

## Delivery

Delivery within Dubai is next day for orders placed before 3 pm. Delivery to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain takes 2 to 3 working days. GCC delivery (Saudi Arabia, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar) takes 5 to 7 working days. Delivery is free on UAE orders over 300 AED. Below 300 AED, UAE delivery costs 25 AED. GCC delivery is a flat 60 AED.

## Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash on delivery is available in the UAE for orders up to 2,000 AED. Customers can also split payments into 4 interest-free instalments with Tabby.

## Sizing and alterations

Our sizes run from XS to 3XL. Every product page has a size chart in centimetres. Basic hem alterations cost 40 AED and take 3 working days. VIP customers (spend over 1,000 AED in 12 months) get free alterations on every order.

## Gift wrapping

Gift wrapping with a handwritten card costs 20 AED and is free during Eid and Ramadan campaigns.

## Contact

Customer care is available on WhatsApp and email, 9 am to 9 pm, seven days a week. Typical reply time is under 2 hours.
