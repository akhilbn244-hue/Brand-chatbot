NOOR FASHION - 2025 CAMPAIGN RESULTS SUMMARY (fictional demo data)
Prepared by the marketing team, January 2026

OVERVIEW
In 2025 we ran four major email and social campaigns. The Ramadan campaign was our best performer by every measure. Email remained our highest-return channel, with an average return on ad spend (ROAS) of 6.2 compared with 3.1 for Instagram ads and 2.4 for TikTok ads.

1. RAMADAN COLLECTION (March 2025)
Email open rate: 48 percent. Click-through rate: 6.1 percent.
Revenue: 412,000 AED. Best-selling product: the Layla kaftan.
What worked: sending the first email at 9 pm, after iftar, doubled the clicks compared with a morning send. Free gift wrapping drove a 22 percent rise in orders marked as gifts.

2. EID AL-FITR (April 2025)
Email open rate: 41 percent. Click-through rate: 4.8 percent.
Revenue: 286,000 AED.
What worked: a countdown timer in the email header. What did not work: a 30 percent discount attracted many one-time buyers who never ordered again.

3. SUMMER EDIT (July 2025)
Email open rate: 29 percent. Click-through rate: 2.2 percent.
Revenue: 74,000 AED.
Lesson: many customers travel in July, so engagement drops. Plan smaller campaigns in summer.

4. WHITE FRIDAY (November 2025)
Email open rate: 37 percent. Click-through rate: 5.3 percent.
Revenue: 351,000 AED.
What worked: giving VIP customers 24-hour early access. VIPs produced 46 percent of White Friday revenue while being only 12 percent of the customer list.

SEGMENT RESULTS ACROSS ALL 2025 EMAILS
VIP customers: open rate 58 percent.
Regular customers: open rate 36 percent.
New customers: open rate 31 percent.

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR 2026
- Keep sending Ramadan emails after iftar.
- Use early access and exclusivity for VIPs instead of deeper discounts.
- Avoid discounts above 25 percent, which attract one-time buyers.
- Run a welcome series of three emails for New customers in their first two weeks.
